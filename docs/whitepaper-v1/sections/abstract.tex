\section*{Abstract}

We present RandAO, a novel decentralized random number generation protocol that achieves unprecedented robustness and liveness through an "any honest" guarantee - ensuring randomness integrity as long as at least one participant remains honest. By integrating Verifiable Delay Functions (VDFs) with a commit-reveal scheme, RandAO prevents denial of service attacks and manipulation attempts while maintaining unpredictable output generation. The protocol's economic security scales with total staked collateral, requiring complete provider set collusion for successful attacks, making it suitable for high-stakes applications like universal lotteries. We introduce the \$RNG token economy that creates a sustainable marketplace for randomness generation, where providers compete on service quality and earn rewards through both direct service payments and staking mechanisms. This dual incentive structure, combined with the protocol's robust security model, establishes a foundation for trustless on-chain randomness.
